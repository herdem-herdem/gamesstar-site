Requesting help

Include the app version, device model and Android version and steps to reproduce the issue. If needed, attach a screenshot with personal information hidden. Do not send passwords, payment card details or unnecessary identity documents. Share support details in the app creates text with the version and catalogue count; it does not send it automatically.

Purchases and refunds

Restore permanent packages with the same Google Account through Coin Shop → Restore purchases. Consumable coins remain on the device and are not synchronized across devices. If delivery is saved but Google Play acknowledgement is unfinished, check your connection and reopen the app or use Retry in the shop. Refund requests use Google Play’s refund process. Real-money sales are not yet enabled in the current Android development build.

Local records and privacy

Paintings save automatically on this device. Continue from My Paintings. Clear all paintings removes progress only, not favorites, preferences or all purchase records. Email us about data rights or child-related privacy requests. The Privacy Policy provides more information. Android cloud and device-transfer backups are disabled; uninstalling the app or clearing storage in Android settings removes local progress. Painting/coin progress from the previous Unity version is not imported into this Android update.
