1. Publisher and scope

This policy describes the Coloring Brawl Stars Skins Android application provided by Selçuk Erdem under the GamesStar name. Privacy and data enquiries: herdem09@pm.me. It reflects the behavior of the Android update as of 26 October 2026.

2. Painting data on your device

Painting progress, completion records, coin balance, earned rewards, favorites, recently opened pictures, language, sound and haptic preferences are stored in this application’s private device storage. There is no user account or feature that uploads your paintings to a developer server. Painting works without an internet connection. Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for this app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes these local records.

3. Advertising and Google

The app uses Google AdMob. Debug builds use Google test ads; release builds use the app’s own ad units. Test ads can also involve network and data processing. When loading ads, Google may process IP addresses and inferred approximate location, device- or app-related identifiers, ad impressions, taps and video interactions, and crash and performance information. Purposes may include ad delivery, measurement and analytics, diagnostics and abuse prevention. Available identifiers depend on device settings, permissions and SDK configuration; for example, an App Set ID may be used. Removing the advertising ID permission does not disable every identifier or all data processing. The app does not request precise location permission. Google services also follow their own privacy terms.

Ad requests use child-protective, non-personalized treatment and a G content limit for every age group. Advertising ID (AD_ID) and Android advertising-services permissions are removed from the app package. There is no fixed bottom banner. Each gallery has at most one native ad placement; the completion dialog has one rectangle placement. Remove Ads disables both placements.

4. Choices and permissions

The Android app does not request precise location, contacts, camera or broad storage access. Saving a picture uses the system media library on Android 10 and newer and the system document picker on Android 7–9. Sharing uses the Android system chooser and grants the selected recipient temporary access to the image.

Before requesting ads, the app checks Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) status. It presents a privacy message when UMP requires it and requests ads only when the SDK permits them. Valid status from a previous session may be used. Where required, Settings → Ads & Tracking lets you change privacy choices. Reading this policy is not advertising consent. Debug builds use Google demo ad IDs; release builds use this application’s own IDs. The protective UMP under-age signal prevents requests for advertising consent from children.

5. Purchases

The optional Coin Shop uses Google Play Billing. Google Play provides available products’ local prices and payment confirmation; the developer does not receive payment card details. Product IDs, purchase tokens and delivery/acknowledgement/consumption retry status are stored locally. A signed Play response is verified before delivery; the same transaction does not grant coins twice. Failed acknowledgement or consumption is retained in a local queue and may be retried while the app runs or when it is reopened.

Real-money sales are not yet enabled in this Android development build. Play products and verification configuration are required before sales are enabled. Coin balances are local and are not synchronized across devices by the app. Restoring permanent entitlements and checking ownership require communication with Google Play.

6. Sharing, pictures and support

A painting is sent to a service you choose only when you use the Android sharing interface and select a destination. On Android 10 and newer, saving to the gallery uses scoped system media access. On Android 7–9, you choose an export destination using the system file picker. No broad storage permission is requested. Saved or shared copies exist independently of app storage.

If you email support, your email address, message and voluntarily attached files are processed to respond, resolve the issue and keep necessary records. Email providers may process the communication. Do not send passwords, payment card details or unnecessary personal information. The app does not automatically send support emails or painting files.

An image prepared for the Android 7–9 document picker is temporarily kept in private app storage so Activity or process recreation does not lose it. It is removed after a successful save or cancellation; a new export may replace a previously pending image. Preparing an export does not send the image to the developer.

7. Purposes and regional legal bases

Local information is used to operate the game, preserve progress and deliver packages and rewards. Support communications are used to handle your request; transaction records prevent duplicate delivery and meet necessary legal obligations. Where applicable law requires a legal basis, these activities may rely on performance of the service, legal obligations or legitimate interests subject to balancing your rights and freedoms. Advertising or tracking that requires permission must obtain that permission. Reading this policy or using the app does not by itself constitute consent to advertising tracking.

8. Retention, deletion and your rights

Local game data remains until you clear it or remove the application’s data through Android settings. Settings → Clear all paintings clears painting progress only; coins, purchase entitlements, favorites and preferences remain. Uninstalling the app or using Android’s Clear storage removes local app data. Cloud and device-transfer backups are disabled for this app. These actions do not erase Google Play transaction records, Google advertising records or copies shared elsewhere.

Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request and meet necessary legal or dispute-related obligations. Providers apply their own retention policies. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability and withdrawal of consent. Contact herdem09@pm.me. You may also complain to the competent data protection authority in your region; rights under Türkiye’s KVKK are preserved where applicable. We cannot remotely access your local files, so you may need to manage device-only information on your device.

9. Recipients, transfers and security

The developer does not collect your paintings on its own server. Google, the sharing destination you choose and email providers may process information for their respective services. These services may process information outside your country; their notices explain their transfer and storage arrangements. Local data is stored in the Android application container. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

10. Children and changes

The minimum target audience includes ages 5 and under. Before ads start, an optional neutral age-group selection is shown; no date of birth is requested. The broad age group is stored only in private device preferences and is not sent to the developer or in ad requests. Users may decline or dismiss the screen; the same child-protective advertising rules apply to every group. Rewarded video is disabled in this version because dismissal within five seconds has not been verified for all live inventory. The normal completion reward remains 2 coins. A parent or guardian may contact us about personal data shared through support.

This policy will be updated if data practices change. Contact: Selçuk Erdem · GamesStar · herdem09@pm.me.
