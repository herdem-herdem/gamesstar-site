About this website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and web request data to operate and secure its service. GamesStar does not add analytics, advertising, cookie, or tracking code to this site. Details: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

1. Publisher and scope

This policy describes Coloring Brawl Stars Skins, published by Selçuk Erdem under the GamesStar name. Privacy contact: herdem09@pm.me. It also covers the development build displayed as B. It describes the application as configured on 18 October 2026.

2. Painting data on your device

Painting progress, completion records, coin balance, earned rewards, favorites, recently opened pictures, sound and haptic preferences are stored in the application’s device storage. There is no user account or feature that uploads your paintings to a developer server. Painting works without an internet connection. Depending on Apple settings, application data may be included in device/system backups; this is not an app-provided cloud synchronization service.

3. Advertising and Google

The app uses Google AdMob. The current development build uses only Google test ads; test ads do not mean that no network or data processing occurs. When loading ads, Google may process IP addresses and inferred approximate location, device- or app-related identifiers, ad impressions, taps and video interactions, and crash and performance information. Purposes may include ad delivery, measurement, diagnostics and abuse prevention. Available identifiers depend on device settings, permissions and SDK configuration. The app does not request precise location permission. Google services also follow their own privacy terms.

4. Choices and permissions

The current test build does not request Apple ATT permission for cross-app tracking; it does not claim to have collected consent for personalized live advertising. Regional consent, age/audience settings and tracking permissions where required must be configured before live advertising. Apple tracking permissions can be managed in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The Remove Ads entitlement removes banners, interleaved ad cards and completion display ads; optional rewarded videos may remain available. This entitlement does not delete previously processed data or mean that all SDK communication stops.

5. Purchases

Apple processes payments; the developer does not receive your card or bank details. To verify entitlements and avoid duplicate delivery, the app stores product identifiers, transaction identifiers and permanent package entitlements locally. Verification and restoration may communicate with Apple. Coin balance is local; consumable coins are not synchronized across devices by the app. Current local StoreKit tests do not charge real money. In a version with products available for sale, Apple presents the actual price and payment confirmation.

6. Sharing, Photos and support

A painting is sent to a service you choose only when you use the sharing interface and select a destination. Saving to Photos may request iOS add-only permission; the app does not read your photo library. Saved or shared copies exist independently of the app’s storage.

If you email support, your email address, message and voluntarily attached files are processed to respond, resolve the issue and keep necessary records. Email providers may process the communication. Do not send passwords, payment card details or unnecessary personal information. The app does not automatically send support emails or painting files.

7. Purposes and regional legal bases

Local information is used to operate the game, preserve progress and deliver packages and rewards. Support communications are used to handle your request; transaction records prevent duplicate delivery and meet necessary legal obligations. Where applicable law requires a legal basis, these activities may rely on performance of the service, legal obligations or legitimate interests subject to balancing your rights and freedoms. Advertising or tracking that requires permission must obtain that permission. Reading this policy or using the app does not by itself constitute consent to advertising tracking.

8. Retention, deletion and your rights

Local game data remains until you clear it or remove the application’s data through iOS. Settings → Clear all paintings clears painting progress only; it does not erase all coins, purchase entitlements, favorites or preferences. Delete App in iOS removes local app data; Offload App may retain it. System backups can be managed separately through Apple settings. This does not erase Apple transaction records, Google records or copies shared elsewhere.

Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request and meet necessary legal or dispute-related obligations. Providers apply their own retention policies. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability and withdrawal of consent. Contact herdem09@pm.me. You may also complain to the competent data protection authority in your region; rights under Türkiye’s KVKK are preserved where applicable. We cannot remotely access your local files, so you may need to manage device-only information on your device.

9. Recipients, transfers and security

The developer does not collect your paintings on its own server. Apple, Google, the sharing destination you choose and email providers may process information for their respective services. These services may process information outside your country; their notices explain their transfer and storage arrangements. Local data is stored in the iOS application container. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

10. Children and changes

The app does not collect age, date of birth or child profiles. Parents or guardians who believe that information about their child was shared through support may contact us. The current test-ad configuration does not establish suitability for a live child-directed release. The target age group and applicable advertising and parental-consent requirements must be settled before release.

If data practices change, this policy and its date will be updated. Material changes and activities requiring new permissions will receive the necessary notice or permission request. Contact: Selçuk Erdem · GamesStar · herdem09@pm.me.
