1. Service

Coloring Brawl Stars Skins is an independent paint-by-number application provided by Selçuk Erdem / GamesStar. Use is subject to applicable law and Google Play terms. This information does not limit mandatory consumer rights.

2. Coins and packages

Coins are virtual units used only for in-app hints; they are not money, an investment, transferable funds or redeemable cash. Packages of 50, 100 and 500 coins are consumable. Unlimited Coins and Remove Ads are one-time permanent entitlements, not subscriptions. Unlimited Coins prevents hints from reducing your balance; it does not change the finite coin balance. Remove Ads removes gallery and completion display ads; it does not necessarily remove optional rewarded videos.

3. Payment, restoration and refunds

For products available for sale, Google Play’s displayed local price and payment confirmation govern the purchase; Google Play processes payment. Restore permanent packages with the same Google Account through Coin Shop → Restore purchases. Consumable coins and local painting progress are not transferred across devices by the app. Refund requests follow Google Play processes and applicable consumer law. Refunded or revoked permanent entitlements may be withdrawn after a successful Play ownership check. Users who require purchase approval must follow Google Play family approval rules.

The app acknowledges or consumes a purchase with Google after verification and a local delivery commit. Connection failures are retained for retry; retrying does not grant coins a second time. For an unfinished transaction, reopen the app or use Retry in the shop. Real-money sales are not enabled in this development build; configured products and Play verification are required before purchases become available.

4. Play, storage and ad rewards

Progress is saved automatically on the device; recovery after deletion or device loss is not guaranteed. First completion of a picture awards 2 coins. Where available, completing an optional video and receiving the ad provider’s reward confirmation awards 2 additional coins. Cancellation, connectivity issues or unavailable ads do not provide the bonus. A picture’s base and bonus rewards are not repeatedly awarded. You can paint the available pictures without purchasing or watching ads. Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for this app. Painting and coin progress from the previous Unity version is not imported into this Android update; the new local progress may start from its initial state. This does not delete Google Play transaction records.

Rewarded video is disabled in this version because dismissal within five seconds is unverified; the base 2-coin reward is unchanged.
